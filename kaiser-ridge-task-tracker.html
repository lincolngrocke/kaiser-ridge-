Backup & Restore
Your directory, checklists, guides, settings, and entries are saved only on this device. The app keeps a fresh backup snapshot automatically every time you save a task or clock out — export it regularly (especially before reinstalling the app or switching phones) and store it somewhere safe like Google Drive, Files, or email it to yourself.
